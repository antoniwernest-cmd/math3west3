STUDENT FLOW
One question fills the screen. Students drag or tap their responses; they do not
type answers. Only the teacher entry/name and teacher tools contain text inputs.
Start voice and Stop voice read the question. A small current-page PNG is available.
Selected choices turn bright orange and grow slightly, regardless of correctness.
3D stars show progress. Lock in your answer saves the response and page PNG, then
advances. Complete wrong answers and wrong models are accepted. Empty required
responses receive a neutral reminder. The assessment does not reveal correct answers.
The last locked response leads to Complete — Please call your teacher.

DRAGGING AND EXCHANGES
Native Math Landing block values, colours and action history are reused. Blocks can
move in both directions on accepted pads and can be placed at the drop position.
Moving one does not rearrange the others. An overlapping drop moves only the dropped
block to the nearest clear position on the same pad. Values and original colours
are retained. Unit cubes are scattered, have a strong shadow and remain readable.
Group and Ungroup controls show larger cats with blue T and red U flags. Exchanges
are recorded with their pad, input value/count and output value/count. Undo reverses
undone exchange evidence, and Reset clears it. Addition cross-pad drag/tap observations
are reported separately from answer scores; Undo does not erase an observed drag.

are real working areas. Large collections on a tablet use a full-width Way switch. Each correct total earns one point. Two different value
combinations making the target earn a third point; changing positions alone does not.
